Get your gi

Loaner gis are available the first week. After that, families buy their own gi with the team patch.

The recommended gi is the Elite Sports kids BJJ gi.

  • IBJJF approved
  • Free white belt included
  • Pre-shrunk pearl weave
  • Machine washable

Order by the Elite Sports size chart below. Measure your child's height and weight. Don't order by their current gi size and don't size up for growth; Elite runs true to size and bigger means sleeves and pants that are too long.
