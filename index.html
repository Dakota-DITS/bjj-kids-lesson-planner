What's grace?

Grace is God's kindness to us that we could never earn. We've all sinned and fall short of His glory (Romans 3:23), and the wages of sin is death. But God showed His love for us in this: while we were still sinners, Christ died for us (Romans 5:8). Jesus, God's only Son, lived a perfect life, gave Himself on the cross as the sacrifice for our sins, and rose again on the third day. Through Him we're redeemed and forgiven (Ephesians 1:7). Salvation is a free gift, received by faith in Jesus and not by anything we do (Ephesians 2:8–9). That's why our program is named Grace.

Want to know more? Ask any of our teachers.
