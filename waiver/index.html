Rules and guidelines, enrollment, and waiver. Ages 11 and up.

Guidelines for BJJ at Grace Baptist Church

  1. Students must sign in and remove their shoes before stepping onto the mat.
  2. Students must be ready for class (belt tied and lined up) five minutes before class starts. If a student is late, he/she must wait outside of the mat until the instructor gives him/her permission to join the class.
  3. Students must greet everyone on the mat, especially the instructor, regardless of rank.
  4. Students must stretch and warm up before each class.
  5. Students must put their cell phones or mobile devices away during class, unless they are expecting an urgent phone call.
  6. Students must keep their GIs clean, dry, and free from any unpleasant odors.
  7. Students must keep finger and toe nails trimmed. Long hair must be tied, and jewelry removed. Makeup, glitter, or strong perfume that can rub off on other people or the mats must be removed before class.
  8. Students must keep a respectful posture during class. Students must sit in seiza when the instructor is demonstrating the techniques.
  9. Students must keep talking to a minimum. Conversation should be related to the class subject and questions must be addressed to the instructor, rather than fellow students.
  10. Students and parents/visitors/observers will refrain from using foul language.
  11. Students must keep their belts on at all times. The belt represents their progress.
  12. Students must wear a rash guard under the GI and must keep shirts on at all times. They must change in the locker room.
  13. Students must notify the instructor and training partner of injuries or physical limitations.
  14. Students must not spar without permission and supervision from the instructor.
  15. Students must be aware of the people and objects around them on the mat, both when training and resting.
  16. Students should stop training/sparring/practicing if they feel discomfort of any kind.
  17. Students must tap out as soon as they are submitted in sparring. Students must also let go of a joint manipulation (i.e., arm bar) or chokes if the partner is defenseless, even if their training partner has not tapped out.
  18. Students must be courteous to fellow students at all times and maintain a friendly and positive attitude. Students should 'check their ego' at the door. Your ego is not your amigo in BJJ. Students must refrain from celebrating or bragging when they submit a training partner. Likewise, students must refrain from complaining when submitted. When a student is submitted, he/she will thank his/her sparring partner for a good challenge, and ask the instructor after class how he/she could have escaped the submission.
  19. Students must not ask fellow students to spar, unless cleared by the instructor. Lower ranking students must never call higher ranking students to spar.
  20. Students must report any problems to the instructor.
  21. Students must ask permission to leave the mat during class for bathroom breaks.
  22. Parents/observers/visitors must not coach their kids.
