Guidelines for kids

  • Shoes come off before you step on the mat.
  • Line up by rank, with your belt tied, and be ready on time.
  • Say hello to everyone on the mat. Kids call instructors “teacher.”
  • Keep nails trimmed, leave jewelry at home, and tie long hair back.
  • Wear a clean gi.
  • Sit and listen when the teacher shows a technique.
  • Use kind words only.
  • Keep your belt on. It shows the work you have done.
  • Tell a teacher if you are hurt or something does not feel right.
  • No sparring unless a teacher says it is okay and stays with you.
  • Tap early. When your partner taps, let go right away.
  • Be a good sport. No bragging when you submit someone, and no complaining when you get submitted. Thank your partner.
  • Ask a teacher before you leave the mat for the bathroom.
  • Parents, visitors, and observers do not coach from the side.

Parents and visitors: keep cell phones and mobile devices put away during class, unless you are expecting an urgent phone call.
