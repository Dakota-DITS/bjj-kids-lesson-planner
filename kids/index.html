    What's grace?

    Grace is God's kindness to us that we could never earn. We've all sinned and fall short of His glory (Romans 3:23), and the wages of sin is death. But God showed His love for us in this: while we were still sinners, Christ died for us (Romans 5:8). Jesus, God's only Son, lived a perfect life, gave Himself on the cross as the sacrifice for our sins, and rose again on the third day. Through Him we're redeemed and forgiven (Ephesians 1:7). Salvation is a free gift, received by faith in Jesus and not by anything we do (Ephesians 2:8–9). That's why our program is named Grace.

    Want to know more? Ask any of our teachers.

    Free, because grace is free.

    Grace Kids BJJ costs nothing, just like God's grace. Our salvation isn't earned by anything we give (Ephesians 2:8–9), yet we're still called to give cheerfully (2 Corinthians 9:7). We ask that you give what you feel called to give, with a suggested minimum of $5 each class if you're able. Every dollar goes toward mat care, equipment, and even helping keep the lights on, so we can serve your kids better. The more we receive, the more we're able to do. If you can't give, that's completely fine. Your family is just as welcome here.

    What to expect at the first class

    • Arrive about 10 minutes early.
    • A loaner gi is available the first week.
    • Bring a water bottle. Come with trimmed nails and no jewelry.
    • Kids call instructors “teacher” and line up by rank.
    • Class opens with a short prayer, then warmup, base movements, technique, and play time.
    • Sparring is only for kids with at least one stripe.

    Age groups

    • 3–6Mighty Mites. Motor skills, balance, listening, and games.
    • 7–10Pee Wees. Partner drills, positions, escapes, and controlled sparring.

    Kids ages 3–6 must be fully potty trained to join class.

    FAQ

    What ages can come?

    Ages 3–10, in two groups: 3–6 Mighty Mites and 7–10 Pee Wees.

    How many adults are in the room?

    We keep a 4:1 kid-to-adult ratio.

    Get your gi

    Loaner gis are available the first week. After that, families buy their own gi with the team patch.

    The recommended gi is the Elite Sports kids BJJ gi.

    • IBJJF approved
    • Free white belt included
    • Pre-shrunk pearl weave
    • Machine washable

    Order by the Elite Sports size chart below. Measure your child's height and weight. Don't order by their current gi size and don't size up for growth; Elite runs true to size and bigger means sleeves and pants that are too long.
